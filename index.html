Applying agentic AI to enterprise client onboarding

I'm most interested in where technology creates lasting competitive advantage, not where it only modernizes legacy systems. Onboarding a new wealth management client is a good example: many teams, strict sequencing, and costs that need to be predictable and measurable.

  1. A manual process

    Building a new client environment was a manual process that depended on tribal knowledge spread across teams.

  2. A standard playbook

    I architected the standardized environment build process, now adopted across 10+ engineering teams. It cut engineering effort per build by 46%.

  3. An orchestration layer

    I designed an agentic AI approach that automates cross-team build sequencing, so onboarding costs become predictable and measurable.

Under this program we delivered concurrent production environment buildouts for multiple enterprise wealth management clients, the fastest client onboarding pace in company history.
